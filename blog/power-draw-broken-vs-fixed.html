Identical work. The broken build reported 100% GPU utilization the entire time it was drawing a third of the card's power budget — it was running the attention kernel on 2 of 170 SMs. Area under each curve is energy: 5.85 kJ broken vs 2.87 kJ fixed, so the misconfigured build burned roughly twice the energy to do the same job 5.9× slower.
